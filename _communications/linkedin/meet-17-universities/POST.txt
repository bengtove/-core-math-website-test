Meet the 17 universities behind CoRE-Math!

CoRE-Math brings together eight African and nine European universities, combining longstanding collaborations with newer partnerships.

Africa
• Addis Ababa University
• Makerere University
• Stellenbosch University — joined in 2026
• University of Dar es Salaam
• University of KwaZulu-Natal
• University of Nairobi
• University of Rwanda
• University of Zambia

Europe
• Linköping University
• LUT University
• Stockholm University
• University of Amsterdam
• University of Bergen
• University of Groningen
• University of Oslo
• University of Vienna
• Uppsala University

As an Africa–Europe Cluster of Research Excellence, CoRE-Math is part of the initiative of the African Research Universities Alliance (ARUA) and The Guild of European Research-Intensive Universities.

Together, we strengthen mathematical research and postgraduate education, develop new collaborations and explore how mathematics can contribute to addressing societal challenges.
