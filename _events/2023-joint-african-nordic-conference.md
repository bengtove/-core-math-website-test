The four-day Joint African–Nordic Conference in Mathematics provided a forum for sharing research findings and reviewing two decades of Nordic support for mathematics in the region. It was Math4SDG’s first African–Nordic conference and predates the establishment of CoRE-Math; its relationship to the network is through the Math4SDG project.
