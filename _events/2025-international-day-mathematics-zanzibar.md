The national International Day of Mathematics celebration in Zanzibar brought together pupils, teachers and education stakeholders to promote mathematics learning and participation. It was organised by the Mathematics Association of Tanzania and is included as a significant activity at a CoRE-Math member node; it is not presented as a CoRE-Math-organised event.
