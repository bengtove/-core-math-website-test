Run4Math8 brought mathematics awareness, mentorship and community engagement to Tororo. The marathon was organised as an International Day of Mathematics activity by AWoM and Busitema University. It is included as an activity of an associated regional mathematics network; it is not presented as a CoRE-Math-organised event.
