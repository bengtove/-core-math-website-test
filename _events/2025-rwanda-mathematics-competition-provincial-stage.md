The provincial second stage of the seventh Rwanda Mathematics Competition involved 950 students, with successful participants progressing to the national stage. The competition is one of the national activities connected through the CoRE-Math Mathematics Competitions programme; this record does not imply that CoRE-Math organised the competition.
