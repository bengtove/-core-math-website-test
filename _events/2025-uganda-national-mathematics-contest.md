The annual Uganda National Mathematics Contest involved primary, secondary, university and vocational students through centres across the country. The 2025 contest included April and June papers, with prizes awarded at Makerere University in July. Uganda’s mathematics network carries out the contest with the Uganda Mathematical Society; this record does not imply that CoRE-Math organised it.
