**Time:** 12:00–14:00 (Amsterdam local time)

An IAS fellowship event presenting a collaborative project by Diletta Martinelli (University of Amsterdam), Jim Portegies (Eindhoven University of Technology) and Michael Oyengo (Maseno University, Kenya). The project explores how Waterproof, the STACK online assessment system and generative AI can support mathematics teaching and learning in Sub-Saharan Africa, with attention to accessibility, collaboration and inclusion.
