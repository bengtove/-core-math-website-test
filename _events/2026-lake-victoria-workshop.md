The Lake Victoria Workshop will bring together mathematical scientists, researchers from other disciplines and regional stakeholders to develop a collaborative research programme addressing scientific and societal challenges in the Lake Victoria Basin.

The workshop will focus on identifying priority research questions, relevant data sources and opportunities for mathematical modelling and data integration. Particular attention will be given to defining a small number of concrete scientific problems that could form the basis of initial collaborative projects.

The workshop is an important step in developing the Lake Victoria Living Lab initiative. Expected outcomes include agreement on initial research priorities, stronger partnerships and a roadmap for collaborative research and future funding applications.
