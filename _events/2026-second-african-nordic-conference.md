The Second African–Nordic Conference in Mathematics was held in Arusha, Tanzania, on 11–14 August 2026 under the theme *Collaborative Research in Mathematics and Mathematics Education for Sustainable Development*. The conference brought together 104 participants from 14 countries.

The conference was organised within the [Mathematics for Sustainable Development (Math4SDG) project]({{ "/projects/math4sdg/" | relative_url }}), a partnership between the University of Dar es Salaam, Makerere University and the University of Bergen, funded by Norad through NORHED II, in collaboration with CoRE-Math, ISP and Sida. IUCEA provided travel grants to participants from its East African member universities.

The scientific programme included keynote and plenary lectures and contributed talks covering pure and applied mathematics, mathematical modelling, mathematical biology, health, environmental sciences, artificial intelligence, data science, statistics, finance and mathematics education. CoRE-Math meetings were held alongside the scientific programme.

The conference was an important concluding activity of the Math4SDG project, building on longstanding African–Nordic collaboration in the mathematical sciences.

The conference also attracted local media attention. Arusha News highlighted research by Bright Elisamehe on mathematical modelling of early blight disease in tomatoes and by Ema Boki on digital learning materials for mathematics education in Tanzanian secondary schools.
