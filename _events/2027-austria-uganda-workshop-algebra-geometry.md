The Austria–Uganda Workshop on Algebra and Geometry brings together mathematicians from the Uganda–Austria collaboration and colleagues from across the region for minicourses and research talks.

The workshop forms part of the [Africa-UniNet Uganda–Austria Collaboration in Algebra and Geometry](https://africa-uninet.at/en/projects/call4/p142-uganda). The collaboration studies questions at the interface of algebra and geometry, including ring theory and module theory, and aims to strengthen research connections and capacity. It involves senior and early-career researchers and PhD students, with mentoring and opportunities to develop teaching and research skills.

The collaboration is coordinated by University of Vienna with Makerere University, Kabale University, Busitema University and Graz University of Technology as partner institutions. The workshop is supported by the Africa-UniNet project, CoRE-Math, EMS CDC, Makerere University and University of Vienna.

The announced programme includes minicourses by Will Donovan (Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University) and Balázs Szendrői (University of Vienna), alongside talks by researchers based at universities in Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania. The official workshop website provides the announced speaker list and further information; it does not currently specify registration arrangements or a detailed timetable.
