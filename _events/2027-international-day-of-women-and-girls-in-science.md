CoRE-Math will mark the International Day of Women and Girls in Science on 11 February 2027 with an event focusing on women in mathematics across its African and European network.

The event will provide an opportunity to present activities undertaken by participating institutions and associated networks, discuss challenges and opportunities for women in mathematical research and education, and identify possible areas for further collaboration.

Further details about the programme and participation will be announced.
