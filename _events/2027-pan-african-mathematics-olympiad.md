The Pan-African Mathematics Olympiad (PAMO) brings together secondary-school students from across Africa for an annual mathematics competition. Organised under the auspices of the African Mathematical Union, PAMO encourages mathematical talent, develops problem-solving skills and promotes mathematical exchange among participating countries.

The 2027 competition will take place in Kigali, Rwanda.
