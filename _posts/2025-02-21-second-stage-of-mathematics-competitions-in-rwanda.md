---
title: "Second stage of Mathematics Competitions in Rwanda"
date: 2025-02-21
type: news
---

On 21st February 2025 the department of Mathematics at the University of Rwanda together with its partners is conducting the Seventh Rwanda Mathematics Competition at Provincial level: second stage. The total number of participants is 950 students. The best from this round will go to the National level. Thanks to management the University of Rwanda College of Science and Technology for the support. Rwandan students taking part in the second stage of the Rwanda Mathematics Competition.
