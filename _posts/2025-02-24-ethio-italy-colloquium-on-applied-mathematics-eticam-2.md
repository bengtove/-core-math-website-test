---
title: "Ethio-Italy Colloquium on Applied Mathematics (ETICAM-2)"
date: 2025-02-24
type: news
---

The Department of Mathematicas at Addis Ababa University organized a school with theme "Classical and New Approaches for Modeling Life Science Problems" from 10th to 11th February 2025 and a workshop" Ethio-Italy Colloquium on Applied Mathematics (ETICAM-2)" in collaboration with Prof. Bruno Buonomo and his colleagues from the Department of Mathematics and Applications, University of Naples Federico II, Italy, from 12th to 13th February 2025. More than 50 mathematicians participated in both the school and the workshop.
