---
title: "Teklemichael Worku successfully defends his PhD in Mathematics."
date: 2025-02-24
type: news
---

Teklemichael Worku defended his PhD Dissertation titled "On Reduced Submodules of Finite Dimensional Polynomial Modules and a Generalization of Torsion Functor" on February 10, 2025 at the Department of Mathematics, Addis Ababa University. He was supervised by Prof. David Ssevviiri from Makerere University, Dr. Nega Arega from Namibia University of Science and Technology and Dr. Tilahun Abebaw from Addis Ababa University. Teklemichael has received financial support from the International Science Programme (ISP).
