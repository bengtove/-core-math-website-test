---
title: "Award for Strong Leadership and Innovation to Dr Celestin Kurujyibwami"
date: 2025-03-04
type: news
---

Today at the College Academic Council Dr. Celestin Kurujyibwami, head of the mathematic department at the University of Rwanda, was awarded the Award for Strong Leadership and Innovation by the Principal of the College of Science, Assoc. Prof. Gatare Ignace. The award recognizes Dr Kurujyibwami leadership in his outreach work through the Rwanda Mathematics Olympiads. Congratulations!
