---
title: "Meeting in Amsterdam dedicated to Collaborations with African Partners"
date: 2025-03-04
type: news
---

In occasion o f the European Conference of Tropical Ecology , some of the African participants come together and share their views on how we could promote and improve collaborations between the Faculty of Science at the University of Amsterdam and researchers in Africa, in view of the emerging Global South Plan . One of the Keynote speakers from the conference, Nokubonga Mgqatsa, gave a presentation about her views and experiences in this topic.
