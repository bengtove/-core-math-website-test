---
title: "Tanzania celebrates International Day of Mathematics"
date: 2025-03-17
type: news
---

Celebrations to mark the International Day of Mathematics in Tanzania were held in Zanzibar on 14th March 2025. Activities included school project competitions. Best performers during the Tanzania Mathematics Olympiad (TAMO) competitions, held in August 2024, received their awards during the celebrations.
