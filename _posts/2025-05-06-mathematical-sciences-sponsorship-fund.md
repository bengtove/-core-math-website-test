title: "Mathematical Sciences Sponsorship Fund"
date: 2025-05-06
type: news
---

Elsevier’s 2025 Mathematical Sciences Sponsorship Fund (MSSF) is now welcoming grant proposals from researchers and organizations worldwide. This year’s theme, Mathematics for All: Cultivating Inclusive Excellence, recognizes the need to create pathways for under-represented groups in mathematics through innovative programs, mentorship and community-building initiatives. Awarded grants cover a one-year period and typically range in value from US$500 to US$4,000. For details, see the [website](https://www.elsevier.com/subject/mathematics/mathematical-sciences-sponsorship-fund).
