---
title: "Online events for ERC Starting Grants for researchers based in Africa"
date: 2025-05-07
type: news
---

The European Research Council (ERC) and EURAXESS Africa are jointly organizing two insightful webinars on "ERC Grants for Researchers based in Africa":

- The French session will take place on 7 May 2025 at 10:00 CEST.
- The English session will be held on 9 May 2025 at 10:00 CEST.

See [here](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/worldwide/africa/events/online-events-erc-grants-researchers-based-africa) for more information and the registration link.
