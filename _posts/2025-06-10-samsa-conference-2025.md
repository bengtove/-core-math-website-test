The Southern Africa Mathematical Sciences Association (SAMSA) 2025 Annual Conference will be hosted by Mulungushi University's Department of Mathematics and Statistics from November 25th to 28th, 2025, at the Avani Victoria Falls Resort in Livingstone, Zambia.
