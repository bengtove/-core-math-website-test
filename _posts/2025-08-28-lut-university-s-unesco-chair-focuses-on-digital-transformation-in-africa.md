---
title: "LUT University's UNESCO Chair focuses on digital transformation in Africa"
date: 2025-08-28
type: news
---

CoRE partner LUT University has been awarded a UNESCO Chair for promoting the digital transformation of society and the economy in Africa. Related projects led by LUT aim to alleviate poverty and advance equal access to digital education and health care services. Read [here](https://www.lut.fi/en/articles/lut-universitys-unesco-chair-focuses-digital-transformation-africa) for more details.
