---
title: "Global South Fellowship Scheme at the University of Amsterdam"
date: 2025-09-10
type: news
---

The Global South Plan of the Faculty of Science is pleased to announce the first Fellowship Scheme for scientists from the Global South. The initiative will support four scientists to visit the faculty for a few months in 2026 to work with a local co-applicant. One fellowship is co-sponsored by CIMPA and it will be dedicated to mathematics. Deadline for application is November 15. Details and application form can be found here .
