---
title: "The Second African-Nordic Conference in Mathematics"
date: 2025-10-14
type: news
---

The second African-Nordic conference in mathematics shall be held at Lush Gardens Hotel, Arusha, Tanzania from 11th to 14th August 2026. For more details visit the conference [website](https://math4sdg.udsm.ac.tz/2026-conference).
