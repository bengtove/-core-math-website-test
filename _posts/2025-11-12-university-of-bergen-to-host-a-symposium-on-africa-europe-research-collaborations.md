---
title: "University of Bergen to host a symposium on Africa-Europe Research Collaborations"
date: 2025-11-12
type: news
---

The University of Bergen will host a symposium on Africa-Europe Research Collaborations , in collaboration with the Coimbra Group and the Southern African–Nordic Centre (SANORD) . This event draws inspiration from the Africa Charter for Transformative Research Collaborations but will also have a broader approach to collaboration in research and education between African and European institutions. The seminar is open to everyone interested and will be streamed for those not able to participate in Bergen (the link will be made available on the seminar website). Follow this link to register by 20th November 2025.
