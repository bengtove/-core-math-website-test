The Second African-Nordic Conference in Mathematics took place in Arusha, Tanzania, from 11 to 14 August 2026. The conference brought together more than 75 participants in mathematics and mathematics education.

![Participants at the Second African–Nordic Conference in Mathematics]({{ "/assets/images/news/560c14_002b06f78f884e469c82ba667dccff3f~mv2.jpg.avif" | relative_url }})

The conference was organised within the Mathematics for Sustainable Development (Math4SDG) project, funded by Norad through NORHED II, in collaboration with CoRE-Math, the International Science Programme (ISP), and the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency (Sida).
