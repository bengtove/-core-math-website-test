The 2026 School on Arithmetic, Geometry, and Algorithms is taking place at Addis Ababa University, Ethiopia, from 16th to 28th August 2026. The school is coordinated by AAU-AMU-CIMPA-CoRE-Math-ICTP-ISP. The main topics of this school are arithmetic and algebraic geometry, with a view toward applications, computational aspects, and modern algorithms.

Specific topics to be covered are:

- Algebraic varieties
- Commutative algebra
- Elliptic Curves
- Algebraic Number Theory
- Finite Fields
- Applications of Arithmetic and Algebraic Geometry in cryptography
- Computer Algebra Systems

For more information see [here](https://indico.ictp.it/event/11125).
