The Makerere University Mathematical Society (MUMS) was officially launched on 2 October 2026. The society aims to transform how mathematics is perceived, used and enjoyed at Makerere University, while promoting innovation and excellence in the mathematical sciences.

![Participants at the launch of the Makerere University Mathematical Society (MUMS).]({{ '/assets/images/news/makerere-mathematical-society-launch-2026.avif' | relative_url }})

*Participants at the launch of the Makerere University Mathematical Society (MUMS).*

The establishment of MUMS followed Pi Day celebrations in Nakaseke District, Uganda, and a call to action from Professor Betty Kivumbi Nannyonga. The society aims to create a hub for mathematical innovation, bringing together people interested in mathematics and encouraging its application to contemporary challenges.

The launch represents a new initiative to strengthen the mathematical community at Makerere University and promote greater engagement with mathematics.
