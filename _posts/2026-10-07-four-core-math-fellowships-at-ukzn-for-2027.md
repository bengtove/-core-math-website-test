CoRE-Math is offering four fully funded fellowships for research visits to the University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN) in South Africa in 2027.

The fellowships are open to PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and established senior researchers from the African CoRE-Math universities. Fellows will spend up to three months at UKZN, working with researchers in the Discipline of Mathematics. The fellowships are fully funded by UKZN and include a monthly stipend, accommodation, return travel, medical insurance and visa costs. Applications from women researchers are strongly encouraged.

The programme offers opportunities for collaboration across a broad range of areas, including algebra, analysis, computational mathematics, differential geometry, fluid dynamics, mathematical physics, numerical analysis and topology.

The research visits will take place between **March and June 2027**. Applicants should indicate the research area in which they would like to work and identify potential collaborators at UKZN. Shortlisted applicants will be invited to develop a more detailed proposal jointly with prospective host researchers.

**Application deadline: 31 October 2026.**

Applications should include a motivation letter, CV, description of current research and proposed work at UKZN, a selected publication list, and a letter of support. Applications should be submitted as a zipped file marked with the applicant's name, with the subject **“CoRE-Math Fellowships”**, to [AmeryG1@ukzn.ac.za](mailto:AmeryG1@ukzn.ac.za).
