Five researchers visiting Uppsala University through the [CoRE-Math Fellowship Programme]({{ '/programmes/core-math-fellowships/' | relative_url }}) met with their hosts and staff from the International Science Programme (ISP), the Department of Mathematics and the Department of Statistics for an informal lunch at the Ångström Laboratory.

![CoRE-Math fellows, their hosts and ISP staff at Uppsala University]({{ '/assets/images/news/core-math-fellows-uppsala-2026.jpg' | relative_url }})

*CoRE-Math fellows, their hosts and ISP staff at Uppsala University, 19 October 2026.*

The visiting fellows include three PhD students and two postdoctoral researchers from Addis Ababa University, Makerere University and the University of Dar es Salaam.

The CoRE-Math Fellowship Programme supports research visits and collaboration between mathematicians at participating African and European universities. The fellowships provide opportunities to develop research partnerships, strengthen existing connections and support the professional development of early-career researchers.
