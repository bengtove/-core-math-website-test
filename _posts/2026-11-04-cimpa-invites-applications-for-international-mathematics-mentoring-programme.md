CIMPA has opened applications for its international mentoring programme, which connects students and early-career mathematicians with experienced researchers. The programme offers guidance on research, scientific writing, career development and international collaboration.

**[CIMPA mentoring programme](https://www.cimpa.info/en/form/application-mentees).** Master’s students, PhD students and researchers within five years of completing their PhD may apply if based in [countries eligible for IMU–CDC support](https://www.mathunion.org/cdc/about-cdc/definition-developing-countries). Applications close on **31 December 2026**.

**[PIMS–CIMPA Africa mentoring programme](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eeRtSMTshEjbB42).** A separate programme offers similar opportunities to graduate students, postdoctoral researchers and junior faculty at African universities and research institutions. Eligible applicants in Africa may apply to both programmes.

CIMPA is a long-standing partner of ISP and CoRE-Math. We encourage eligible researchers across the CoRE-Math network to explore these opportunities.

**Further information and applications:** Consult the [official CIMPA announcement](https://www.cimpa.info/en/node/8292) for programme details and application forms.
