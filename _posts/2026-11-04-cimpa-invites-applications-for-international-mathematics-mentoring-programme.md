CIMPA has opened applications for mentoring in the mathematical sciences, connecting students and early-career researchers with experienced researchers. The programme supports scientific writing, funding applications, career planning and international academic connections, with matching informed by research interests, region and language.

**CIMPA programme.** Master's students, PhD students and researchers within five years of their PhD may apply if based in countries or regions on the [IMU-CDC eligibility list](https://www.mathunion.org/cdc/about-cdc/definition-developing-countries). Mentors and mentees commit to one year, renewable by mutual agreement, with approximately one hour of virtual meetings each month. Mentees prepare for meetings, agree goals and may seek occasional feedback on manuscripts, CVs and applications. Apply through the [CIMPA mentee application form](https://www.cimpa.info/en/form/application-mentees) **before 31 December 2026**.

**PIMS–CIMPA Africa programme.** The Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences separately invites graduate students, postdoctoral researchers and junior faculty at African universities and research institutions to connect with mentors at PIMS institutions. The initial, renewable commitment is one year, normally with one to two hours of virtual meetings monthly, preparation, follow-up and an orientation. Use the [PIMS–Africa mentee application form](https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eeRtSMTshEjbB42); **no deadline is stated** in its introduction or CIMPA's announcement. Applicants based in Africa may apply to both programmes.

CIMPA is a long-standing partner of ISP and CoRE-Math. These opportunities are relevant to eligible students and researchers across CoRE-Math member institutions seeking academic guidance and wider research networks.
