A programme bringing mathematics students together with researchers and industry representatives began at the University of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, on 18 November and continues until 4 December 2026.

Organised through [Mathematics for Sustainable Development (Math4SDG)]({{ '/projects/math4sdg/' | relative_url }}), the programme combines training in Python, data analysis and machine learning with practical work on problems proposed by industry representatives.

During the first phase, 18–23 November, 25 students are developing their computational skills and working in groups on industry challenges. The activities are coordinated locally by Eunice Mureithi, the Math4SDG coordinator at the University of Dar es Salaam, with researchers from the University of Bergen, including Morten Brun, participating in the programme.

Math4SDG is a NORHED II project funded by Norad and involving the University of Dar es Salaam, Makerere University and the University of Bergen.

The programme continues on 3–4 December with a Mathematics–Industry Workshop, where students will present their findings and participants will explore opportunities for further collaboration, including research projects, internships and student projects.

The initiative is closely connected to [CoRE-Math’s Industrial Mathematics programme]({{ '/programmes/study-groups-with-industry/' | relative_url }}), which brings mathematicians and students together with external partners to address practical challenges through mathematical and computational methods.
