The African Research Universities Alliance (ARUA), with support from the Mastercard Foundation, has launched the third call for its Early Career Research Fellowship Programme.

The programme offers 40 six-month fellowships for early-career African researchers at institutions associated with ARUA’s Centres of Excellence and the Africa–Europe Clusters of Research Excellence established by ARUA and The Guild of European Research-Intensive Universities.

The fellowships, commencing in May or June 2027, provide opportunities for research, international collaboration and mentorship. At least 70% of the fellowships will be awarded to women.

CoRE-Math encourages eligible early-career researchers in the mathematical sciences to apply.

**Application deadline:** 15 February 2027.

Further information, eligibility criteria and application procedures are available on the ARUA website.
