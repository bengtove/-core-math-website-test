# Women in Math

Women in Math provides a framework for activities aimed at strengthening the participation and development of women in mathematics across the CoRE-Math network. It connects existing initiatives in areas such as research, networking, mentoring, career development and outreach, while providing a basis for developing new activities across the network.

## Activities and networks

### [EANWoBAS — Eastern Africa Network for Women in Basic Sciences](https://www.uu.se/en/centre/international-science-programme-isp/isp-core-programme/isp-and-gender/eanwobas)

EANWoBAS brings together students and staff in mathematics and other basic sciences at universities across Eastern Africa. Through networking, mentorship, outreach and school visits, it works to strengthen the participation of women in the basic sciences. The network has received support from the International Science Programme (ISP) since 2019.

## Related networks and initiatives

The following external networks and initiatives are relevant to this area of work but are not CoRE-Math programmes, projects or formal partners.

### [African Women in Algebra](https://sites.google.com/view/africanwomeninalgebra)

African Women in Algebra is an African research network for women working in algebra and its applications. Its research-oriented activities include workshops, seminars and collaborative research groups.

### [African Women in Mathematics Association (AWMA)](https://www.africanwomeninmath.org/)

AWMA is a pan-African organisation promoting the participation and development of women in mathematics through networking, mentoring and support for study and research careers.

### [STEM MentHER](https://www.stemmenther.co.za/about)

STEM MentHER is a South African mentoring initiative supporting female secondary-school learners interested in STEM and connecting them with women in academia and industry. It was founded by mathematician Cerene Rathilal, who is based at the University of KwaZulu-Natal.
