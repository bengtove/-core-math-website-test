# Sida-supported collaboration

Long-term Sida-supported bilateral research collaboration in mathematics has contributed to postgraduate education and research capacity in several CoRE-Math countries, particularly Rwanda, Uganda and Tanzania, working with Swedish university partners.

The collaboration has included PhD and MSc training, supervision and research collaboration, curriculum development, scientific meetings and regional networking. It is not a single conventional project, but a long-term set of bilateral and regional collaborations that form part of the background from which CoRE-Math has developed.
