# SPIRIT

**Inference for Small Area Estimation through Generative Methods: A Focus on Gender Disparities (SPIRIT)** is a collaboration between researchers at the University of Geneva and the University of Rwanda. The project was awarded **CHF 453,270** by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) under its SPIRIT programme and runs for **43 months from June 2025**.

The project develops new statistical inference methods to improve the reliability of Small Area Estimation (SAE), with particular attention to accurate confidence intervals for data used in policymaking. The methods will be applied to questions concerning gender disparities in health, employment and income in Rwanda. The project will also develop open-source tools for evidence-based decision-making.

Capacity building is an important component. **Three PhD students from the University of Rwanda** are involved and receive support for research training and international mobility. The project therefore contributes both to research collaboration and to postgraduate education within the wider CoRE-Math network.
