# East Africa mathematics events audit 2024–2025

**Updated:** 21 October 2026
**Scope:** Research into historically significant mathematics activities in East Africa for possible inclusion in the CoRE-Math Events collection. This document distinguishes event-level evidence from general references in the ISP annual reports.

Four verified records have now been added to the Events collection:

## Zanzibar date clarification

The ISP Annual Report 2025 records an International Day of Mathematics activity in Zanzibar on **14 March 2025**, with participation by mathematics-network members. Independent local reporting records the national celebration at Mapinduzi Square on **19 March 2025**. The new Event record represents the specifically dated national celebration on 19 March. It does not treat the two references as interchangeable or claim that the 14 March activity was the same event.

## Additional 2025 report findings

The report identifies no additional East African mathematics events with enough date-and-venue information for immediate Event records. It does document related outreach, including Tanzania robotics boot camps and AWoM visits to schools, but these lack sufficient core metadata. They should be researched separately if a broader outreach inventory is desired.

## Event data structure

No data-structure change is proposed. Every added record has an exact date or date range. The current `start_date` and optional `end_date` fields should not be used to simulate an unknown day. Where only a month and year are reliable, retain the activity in this audit until a supported approximate-date representation is deliberately designed and approved.
