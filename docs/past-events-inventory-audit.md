# Past Events inventory audit

**Audit date:** 20 October 2026
**Scope:** Activities from 2024 onward, plus selected earlier activities that are material to the history of a current programme, project or research group. This is a repository audit only: it does not independently verify external sources or create Event records.

## Summary

The Events collection currently contains 14 records. Six are historical as of the audit date:

- four EAALG workshops (2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025);
- the 2026 School on Arithmetic, Geometry and Algorithms; and
- the 2026 Kampala Study Group with Industry.

The collection therefore represents EAALG well, but omits several clearly documented activities from the current CoRE-Math period. The strongest additions are the 2024 and 2025 EAUMP–ICTP schools, the 2025 East African Mathematical Olympiad, the June 2026 Kampala contact workshop, and the 2026 African–Nordic Conference.

This audit examined 45 news posts, all six programme pages, six project pages, four research-group pages, the Education and Outreach pages, the Previous schools archive, and the Events collection. It intentionally excludes routine seminars, individual degree defences, grant announcements, and internal partnership meetings.

## Exclusions and duplicate controls

- The European-partners kick-off meeting in Uppsala (14–15 October 2024) is documented in the News Archive, but is an internal partnership meeting and should remain excluded under this audit’s scope.
- Monthly or individual seminars, including AECC online seminars, NITheCS seminars and the February 2026 UKZN seminar, are routine seminar activity and should not become Past Events records.
- Individual PhD defences, graduations, awards, fellowship announcements and grant announcements are newsworthy but are not Events for this register.
- The Previous schools page is a valuable separate historical archive. Its 2004–2020 records should not be migrated wholesale merely to enlarge Past Events. The 2024 and 2025 schools are recommended because they fall within, or immediately precede, the active CoRE-Math period and have complete core metadata.
- Future activities mentioned in News, such as the December 2026 AIMS Senegal optimal-transport school, are outside a Past Events audit and should be assessed separately for the Events collection.

## Recommendations

1. Add the five confirmed missing events first, using the indicated programme/project associations and retaining the original News or historical-school pages as supporting material.
2. Verify the ten uncertain entries through their linked announcements or programme coordinators before deciding whether to include them. Do not infer CoRE-Math support merely because an event appeared in the News Archive.
3. Reconcile the Kampala SGI Event record’s missing end date with the documented 21–25 September 2026 duration. This is a metadata-completeness issue, not a missing Event.
4. Establish an authoring check for future news posts: when a post reports a dated, significant activity, decide at publication time whether an Events record is also required and record the relationship metadata then.
5. Keep the Previous schools archive as the comprehensive pre-2024 history. Use the Events collection for selected historical records only when they are needed for a current programme, project or research-group timeline.
