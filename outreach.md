# Outreach

CoRE-Math works with schools, teachers and mathematics organisations to promote mathematics and identify and support mathematical talent.

## Mathematics competitions

CoRE-Math organises and supports mathematics competitions to stimulate young people's interest in mathematics, encourage further study—particularly in mathematics and related disciplines—and identify and develop mathematical talent. [Read more about mathematics competitions]({{ '/outreach/mathematics-competitions/' | relative_url }}).

## Schools and young people

CoRE-Math partners work with schools and young people through school visits, mathematics camps and other activities that encourage interest in mathematics and further study in mathematics and science.

## Teacher and coach training

Workshops and training activities support mathematics teachers and competition coaches, including the preparation of students for national, regional and international mathematics competitions.
