# CoRE-Math Fellowships at Uppsala University

The CoRE-Math Fellowships at Uppsala University support research visits by PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and senior researchers from African CoRE-Math universities. Fellows visit Uppsala University to work with researchers in the Department of Mathematics and develop mathematical research collaboration within the CoRE-Math network. The fellowships are funded by Uppsala University, and the International Science Programme (ISP) supports the practical organisation of the visits.
